### Problem-Solving: The Date Conversion Challenge

**The Problem**: Initially, the AI was extracting dates in inconsistent formats ("tomorrow", "Friday", "next week") and even when converting to DD-MM-YYYY, calculations were often off by one day.

**My Approach**:
1. **Researched**: Tested multiple prompt variations to understand the AI's date calculation behavior
2. **Analyzed**: Discovered the AI struggled with day-of-week to date mapping
3. **Innovated**: Created a hybrid solution where JavaScript calculates exact dates for each weekday
4. **Implemented**: Provided these explicit date mappings to the AI with instructions to use exact values
5. **Validated**: Tested with 8+ different scenarios to ensure 100% accuracy

**Result**: Eliminated all date conversion errors, demonstrating my ability to debug AI behavior and find creative solutions.
